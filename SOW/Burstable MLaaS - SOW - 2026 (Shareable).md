1. Engagement Title: Burstable MLaaS
2. Project Scope

LTI will design, build, deploy, and support an AWS-based GPU environment to support GE
Vernova ARC's surrogate modeling workloads.

The project will begin with the deployment of a static AWS GPU environment modeled after GE
Vernova's existing on-premises MLaaS environment. LTI will then work toward an On-Demand
MLaaS Proof of Concept through the end of 2026.

GE Vernova will provide the surrogate modeling workloads and application requirements. LTI will
provide the cloud infrastructure and architecture required to support those workloads.

Timeline & Deliverables

September 2026

●​ Establish access to 3 static servers with 24 total GPUs within an agreed-upon AWS data
center for approximately four ARC researchers.
●​ Enable file transfer between the AWS environment and GE Vernova on-premises
resources.
●​ Provide basic command-line, shell, and script-based access.

4. Hours of service, travel and living expenses
Service will be provided during normal business hours, Monday through Friday. Some
work may need to be performed outside of normal business hours; however, total work should
not exceed 40 hours per week per person. Work performed in excess of 40 hours per week will
be counted as "flex-time," allowing the Agency resource to take an equivalent amount of time as
vacation or saved time.

Any business-related travel requiring reimbursement must be approved by the Client prior to
incurring expenses. The Agency shall provide the Client with an estimated expense as part of the
travel authorization process. The Agency shall adhere to all Client travel policies, and all travel
will be governed by the terms outlined in the Agreement. The Agency shall invoice the Client
once per calendar month for actual, approved travel and living expenses.
5. Out of Scope Work
If the Client desires a change in the Scope of Services, Client shall submit to Agency a
written proposal specifying the desired change(s). Agency will review such proposal at its
standard rates and charges. ​

Agency shall submit to Client a written response to the proposal within fourteen (14) days
following receipt of the proposal. Agency’s written response shall include a statement of the
availability of Agency’s personnel and resources, as well as any impact the proposal will have on
the contract price, deliverable timelines, project completion, and other provisions of this
Agreement. ​
​
​
​
​
Any changes to the Scope of Services shall be evidenced by a “Work Scope
Amendment.” The Work Scope Amendment shall be signed by authorized representatives of
Client and Agency, and shall be deemed part of this Agreement. If Agency does not approve the
Work Scope Amendment, Agency shall not be obligated to perform additional services.
6. Term & Termination
​
This agreement will start on 8/12/2026 and remain in effect until 1/30/2027, at which
point both parties will evaluate the work completed and determine if an additional statement of
work is required.

Either party may terminate this agreement at any time by providing thirty (30) days' prior
written notice to the other party. Such notice must specify the effective date of termination. Upon
termination, both parties agree to cooperate in good faith to wind down services and complete
any outstanding deliverables or transition activities, as agreed upon. Termination shall not relieve
either party of any obligations or liabilities that have accrued prior to the effective date of
termination.
