## Page 1

LTI will provide Cloud Infrastructure Engineering services to support GE Vernova Advanced
Research Center's AWS and cloud infrastructure initiatives.

The initial focus of the engagement will be the design, build, deployment, and support of the
AWS infrastructure required for the Burstable MLaaS environment and the specific deliverables
outlined below.

In addition to these identified deliverables, LTI resources may support other related cloud
infrastructure initiatives and AWS projects based on GE Vernova priorities and requirements. This
may include additional HPC, MLaaS, compute, infrastructure, and cloud architecture efforts,
including initiatives such as Burstable HPC with THOR+.

The deliverables outlined below represent the initial areas of focus for the engagement and are
not intended to limit LTI resources from supporting other related Cloud Infrastructure Engineering
work requested by GE Vernova during the term of this SOW.

Timeline & Deliverables

Phase 1 – Design, Build & Deploy (September – October 2026)

●​ Establish access to 3 static servers with 24 total GPUs within an agreed-upon AWS data
center for approximately four ARC researchers.
●​ Enable file transfer between the AWS environment and GE Vernova on-premises
resources.
●​ Provide basic command-line, shell, and script-based access.
●​ Configure the static servers with the technology stack required to align with the
Alpharetta on-premises MLaaS environment.
●​ Configure the environment to meet applicable GE Vernova cybersecurity and
authentication requirements.
●​ Install additional software and packages required for surrogate model training.
●​ Demonstrate successful surrogate model training equivalent to the on-premises MLaaS
environment across multiple GPUs.
●​ Design, build, and deploy the infrastructure required to support the Burstable MLaaS
offering for ARC.

Phase 2 – Support & Enhance (November – December 2026)

●​ Support and troubleshoot bugs or errors experienced by users within the GPU
environment.
●​ Continue to enhance and support the Burstable MLaaS environment based on
requirements provided by the GE Vernova application team.
